Advanced FIFA Player Visualizations

Interactive D3.js visualizations exploring 17,000+ FIFA 22 players across 6 continents, 162 nations, and 700+ clubs

1. Player Relationship Network

A force-directed graph of the top 80 FIFA players. Blue links connect teammates (same club), orange links connect players of the same nationality. Drag nodes to explore connections. Hover for details.

2. Club Quality Treemap

A zoomable treemap showing the top 3,000 players organized by continent and club. Rectangle size represents the number of top players in each club. Color intensity shows average overall rating. Click to zoom, click the header to zoom out.

3. Wage vs Overall Rating

Each dot represents one of the top 500 rated players. The x-axis shows their overall rating, the y-axis shows their weekly wage in EUR. Brush (click and drag) to select a region and see player details. Dots are colored by continent.

4. Position Distribution by Age

A stacked area chart showing how many players are in each position group (GK, DEF, MID, FWD) at each age. Hover to see exact counts. Notice how forward positions tend to peak at younger ages while defenders and goalkeepers maintain numbers longer.

5. Position Versatility Chord Diagram

This chord diagram reveals how often player positions co-occur. A thick ribbon between ST and CF means many players can play both. Hover over a position arc to highlight its connections, or hover over a ribbon to see the exact count. This reveals the most versatile position pairings in the game.

6. Continent Attribute Radar Comparison

A radial chart comparing the average attribute ratings of players from each continent. Each colored polygon represents a continent. Toggle continents on/off by clicking the legend. Hover over data points for exact values.

7. Attribute Distribution by Continent

Each dot represents a player's rating for the selected attribute. Distributions are spread vertically to avoid overlap, revealing the shape and spread of each continent's player pool. Toggle between attributes to compare. The horizontal spread shows how concentrated or dispersed each continent's talents are.
