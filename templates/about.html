FIFA Player Visualization


Authors: Christopher Pope, Jagnoor Singh and Rachel Kerr


Purpose

Analysis of the FIFA 22 complete player dataset with 17,000+ players.


Home Page

This page shows several interactive visuals of FIFA players:

  1. An interactive D3 circle-packing graph showing the top 2,000 players grouped by continent and country. Click to zoom into continents and countries, hover for player stats.
  2. A horizontal bar graph of the top 10 player-producing countries.
  3. A bar graph of the top 15 highest-paid FIFA players by weekly wage in Euros.
  4. A bar graph of player count by age, color-coded by age group.
  5. A pie chart of clubs with the most players in the top 30.

Comparison Page

This page shows player attribute comparisons via a spider/radar chart. Select any player from the dropdown to see their Overall, Pace, Passing, Physic, Shooting, Defending, and Dribbling stats displayed as a radar chart alongside a stats table.


About Page

This page provides an overview of the project purpose, pages, and team members.


Data Source

FIFA 22 Complete Player Dataset — 17,000+ players with full attributes, sourced from SoFIFA.
